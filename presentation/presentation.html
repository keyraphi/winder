What are "Winding Numbers"?

Winding Number at position $\bm{x}$: $w(\bm{x})$

"Fraction of signed angle subtended at $\bm{x}$."

"How many times does a curve wind counterclockwise around $\bm{x}$."

Line integral along $C$, accumulating differential signed angles.

$$w(\bm{x}) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{\mathcal{C}} \frac{(\bm{y}-\bm{x})^\perp \cdot d\bm{y}}{\|\bm{y}-\bm{x}\|^2}$$

Properties of Winding Numbers

  • Harmonic Field: Field is harmonic $\nabla^{2}w(\mathbf{x})=0$ everywhere outside the curve boundary.
  • Superposition: Winding numbers of individual facets sum linearly to the full curve:
    $w_\Omega(\mathbf{p}) = \sum_i w_{\gamma_i}(\mathbf{p})$
  • Open Boundaries & Holes: Smooth harmonic decay and continuous transition across boundary gaps.
  • Duplicate / Inverted Facets: Opposite segment orientations cancel out cleanly ($1 - 1 = 0$).
