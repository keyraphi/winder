What is a "Winding Number"

Intuitive Definition

  • Number of Times a closed curve travels counterclockwise around a given point.
  • Depends on orientation. Negative for clockwise turns.
  • Robust Topological Invariant: Produces an exact integer ($w \in \mathbb{Z}$) for any closed loop, regardless of local self-intersections or backtracking.

The Generalized Winding Numbers

  • Generalized Winding Number at position $\bm{x}$: $w(\bm{x})$ Fraction of signed angle subtended at $\bm{x}$.

  • Line integral along $C$, accumulating differential signed angles.

    $$w(\bm{x}) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{\mathcal{C}} \frac{(\bm{y}-\bm{x})^\perp \cdot d\bm{y}}{\|\bm{y}-\bm{x}\|^2}$$

Jacobson et al. (2013) — Robust inside-outside segmentation using generalized winding numbers. ACM TOG.

Properties of Winding Numbers

  • Harmonic Field: Field is harmonic $\nabla^{2}w(\mathbf{x})=0$ everywhere outside the curve boundary.
  • Superposition: Winding numbers of individual facets sum linearly to the full curve:
    $w(\mathbf{p}) = \sum_i w_{_i}(\mathbf{p})$
  • Open Boundaries & Holes: Smooth harmonic decay and continuous transition across boundary gaps.
  • Duplicate / Inverted Facets: Opposite segment orientations cancel out cleanly ($1 - 1 = 0$).

Generalized Winding Numbers in 3D

Continuous formulation in 3D

$$\mathcal{W}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{4\pi} \iint_{\mathcal{M}} \frac{(\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{x}) \cdot n(\mathbf{p})}{\|\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{x}\|^3} \, dA(\mathbf{p})$$

Discrete formulation

Dipole (PointNormal) $$\mathcal{W}_{\text{points}}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{4\pi} \sum_{i} \frac{(\mathbf{p_i} - \mathbf{x}) \cdot n_i }{ \|\mathbf{p_i} - \mathbf{x}\|^3} a_i$$

Discrete formulation Triangles

$$\mathcal{W}_{\text{mesh}}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{4\pi} \sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} \Omega_t(\mathbf{x})$$

Complexity in 3D

$$\begin{align*} \mathcal{W}_{\text{mesh}}(\mathbf{x}) &= \frac{1}{4\pi} \sum_{t \in \mathcal{T}} \Omega_t(\mathbf{x}) & \Omega_t(\mathbf{x}) &= 2 \arctan\left(\frac{a \cdot (b \times c)}{\|a\|\|b\|\|c\| + (a \cdot b)\|c\| + (b \cdot c)\|a\| + (c \cdot a)\|b\|}\right) \end{align*} $$

Fast Winding Numbers

1. Exact Input Geometry

Complex, mesh with $N$ segments. Direct evaluation requires $\mathcal{O}(NM)$ operations for $M$ query points.

2. Hierarchical Spatial Acceleration Structure

Build some kind of BVH.

3. Tailor Series Precomputation

Each node $K$ stores geometry dependent taylor coefficients centered at centroid $\tilde{p}$:

$$\begin{align*} \tilde{w}(q) \approx & \textcolor{a51e37}{\left( \sum_{i \in K} a_i \hat{n}_i \right)} \cdot \nabla G(q, \textcolor{0069aa}{\tilde{p}}) + \textcolor{a51e37}{\left( \sum_{i \in K} a_i (p_i - \textcolor{0069aa}{\tilde{p}}) \otimes \hat{n}_i \right)} \cdot \nabla^2 G(q, \textcolor{0069aa}{\tilde{p}}) + \textcolor{a51e37}{\frac{1}{2} \left( \sum_{i \in K} a_i (p_i - \textcolor{0069aa}{\tilde{p}})^{\otimes 2} \otimes \hat{n}_i \right)} \cdot \nabla^3 G(q, \textcolor{0069aa}{\tilde{p}}) \end{align*}$$

4. Traversal & Far-Field Pruning

For query point $q$, evaluate Acceptance Criterion of node: $\|q-\tilde{r}\|_2 < \beta\cdot\tilde{r}$.
Near-field (keep going) Far-field (Approximate) Not visited at all

5. $\mathcal{O}(M \log N)$ Continuous Field

Fast, highly accurate, smooth winding field with open/closed surface robustness.
